# ADR-028: Cargo grids, the boxes that stand on them, and the fitting list

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-10
- Deciders: owner ("another addition to the ship pages would be a visualization of the cargo grid for each ship and what boxes in what configurations will fit, and even more importantly what ships and vehicles can fit in the hangars or storage areas of each ship; the redditor has a great list that he keeps updated with each patch"; then, on the list's standing against CLAUDE.md section 2, "you can trust the redditor's measurements and fit, I have validated them", 2026-11-10), Claude Code session 2026-11-10
- Amends: ADR-012 (the ship file's sections), ADR-023 (the daily refresh's steps)

## Context

The ship file stated a ship's cargo in one figure, the SCU of its particulars. The owner wants two more things: each ship's cargo grids drawn with the boxes that fit them, in the manner of Erec's "Cargo Grid Reference Guide" (an isometric sheet of every vehicle's grids, on the RSI Community Hub); and, for every ship with a hangar or a vehicle bay, which ships and ground vehicles fit inside it, as Pegaxsus's "Ship Fitting List" on r/starcitizen records per patch (rows of vehicles against columns of carriers, with fits, tight fits and failures marked).

What the records hold:

- The vehicle's `cargo_grids` (`VehicleStats.raw`, as the Wiki API serves it): each grid's width, height and length in metres, its SCU, whether it is open to space, and the largest box it takes. 119 of the pack's 253 ships have grids, 410 in all; every grid's volume is its SCU times 1.25 m cubed, so the SCU cell is 1.25 m on every ship; the grids' SCU sum to the ship's cargo figure on every ship. The record's `cargo_limits` repeats the largest box; its `min_size` and `max_size` triples are per-axis caps whose axes do not follow the grid's own, so they are not read.
- The cargo items (`/api/items?filter[type]=Cargo`): 1,475 commodity container records for the build, 1,294 of them named for a size (`…_16SCU_…`) with cargo dimensions; they agree on seven shapes: 1 SCU 1.25 m cubed; 2 SCU 1.25 × 1.25 × 2.5 m; 4 SCU 2.5 × 1.25 × 2.5; 8 SCU 2.5 cubed; 16, 24 and 32 SCU 2.5 × 2.5 by 5, 7.5 and 10 m long. One record named for 4 SCU is a corpse container of another shape.
- Nothing in any record says what fits in a hangar or a vehicle bay: no bay dimensions, no door sizes. The fitting list is tested in play by its author, patch by patch, and kept in a Google Sheet.

CLAUDE.md section 2: every stat comes from the pipeline and nothing is hand-entered; a chat instruction that conflicts with the file is said so and asked about. It was, and the owner decided.

## Decision

| Concern | Choice | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The section | A tenth numbered section, "Cargo", after Crew and operations, on every ship file; a ship whose record lists no grid says so (and what the record states in SCU, if anything), a concept ship says the data is not in the game yet | The same sections for every ship (ADR-012) |
| The facts | Capacity (the grids' SCU), the grids (count, SCU and dimensions, identical grids counted together), the largest box the record says the ship takes, and which grids are open to space | The record's own figures |
| The figure | "Fig. D": each grid as a wire box in cells of one SCU, seen from a corner, in ink on the page's paper (ADR-020), holding boxes of one standard size chosen from a picker; a line per grid saying how many stand and how; a table of every size against every grid; the caption naming the rule and what is not modelled | The owner's example as a drawing from the records, nothing copied from the guide; one accent, ink and paper, like the holomodel and the damage path |
| The arithmetic | `fitOf` in `apps/web/lib/cargo.ts`: boxes upright, in rows across the width and along the length, in as many layers as the height takes, turned a quarter on the floor where that holds more, and only sizes between the smallest and largest box the record names for the grid. One floor division per axis, tested on the pack | A count anyone can check against the drawing; no packing search, no guess at the game's placement |
| What is not modelled, and said so | The game's own placement, mixed loads of several sizes, doors and loading access, and anything tested in play | A reader must not take the drawing for a loading plan; the caption lists them |
| The box shapes | `fixtures/cargo/CargoBox.json`, one row per size, read from the cargo item records by `scripts/bootstrap-fixtures/cargo-boxes.ts` (eight requests through the bootstrap's rate-limited client); a record named for a size whose volume is not that size is left out and counted; two standard shapes for one size halt the run. The daily refresh runs the script after a rebuild, or when the file is missing or for another build | The shapes are game data and come from records, not from a guide or memory; the pipeline's Phase 1 normalizer takes the step over when it reads containers from the game files |
| The fitting list | Decided here and built the same day (the amendment below): the ship file shows, for a carrier, the vehicles that fit it, and for a vehicle, the ships that carry it, from Pegaxsus's list, copied by script from its spreadsheet into `fixtures/fitting/`, never typed in. It is community-tested data and is labelled as such on the page with its author, its patch and a link, never as a game stat, and it is shown only once the author has agreed to the reuse, as with UEX's prices (ADR-013). The owner validated the list's measurements in play and accepted it over section 2's rule for this one source, recorded here as the exception | The owner's decision, the only source there is, and the work of one person whose consent and credit the project owes |

## Amended 2026-11-10: the fitting list, built

The owner sent the sheet's address the same day. What the sheet holds, read from its export as a workbook (the CSV export loses the colours the legend lives in): one "COMBINED SHEET" with a heading row of 61 carriers plus six under "UNRELEASED", a marker row under it (H for a hangar, C for a cargo bay or garage, "w/CM" for the Galaxy's cargo module, an "x" under the Tyilui) that splits the Carrack, the Starlancer, the 890 Jump and the Polaris into two columns, 71 ship rows and 26 ground-vehicle rows in two sections, then rows the sheet keeps about the carriers ("FITS A FABRICATOR?", "SHIP SERVICES") and rumoured ships; a legend whose meaning is the cell colour (green a good fit, orange needs tricks or barely fits, purple-grey fits but bugged, red does not fit, pink not tested) with marks written into some cells (asterisks for wheel and tractor-beam tricks, E, UD, S and N for landing on the 890 Jump, and a few the legend does not explain: SRC, Front); and a "LAST UPDATE" date.

| Concern | Choice | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reading | `scripts/fitting/fetch.ts` reads the workbook cell by cell with `scripts/fitting/xlsx.ts` (a small reader of cells and solid fills, on `fflate`): the verdict of every cell is its colour as the sheet's own legend defines it, read from the legend, not hard-coded; the mark is the text as written, with the legend's words kept where it has them; a cell's tick or cross stands in only where it has no colour. The heading, the markers, the sections and the date are found by their own words; anything missing halts the run | Nothing typed in, and the sheet's meaning taken from the sheet |
| Names | The sheet's headings and row labels are matched to the pack's names by rule (exact, variants where the label says so, the MISC prefix aside) and by `scripts/fitting/names.json` for the rest, kept by hand and reviewed when the sheet gains a row; a name the pack lacks (the Liberator, the Galaxy, the rumoured ships) keeps an empty list and the page shows it as text. A name another row names exactly is not a variant of this row | The one hand-kept file is a lookup, not data |
| On the page | In the Cargo section, after the grids: "Carries" for a ship the sheet has as a carrier, grouped by verdict with each vehicle's space and mark; "Fits in" for any ship or vehicle the sheet has as a row, grouped the same way; the fabricator row as a sentence; under them the title, author, build, date and link, the notice that it is community-tested and not game data, and the sheet's own words for each verdict; an entry in the file's sources | The owner's ask; the labelling the exception requires |
| The author's agreement | The owner directed, the same day, "forget the agreement, assume we have it": the fixture's `source.consent` is recorded as granted on the owner's word, and `docs/legal/attribution.md` says so. The earlier row's "shown only once the author has agreed" is superseded; the credit, the build, the date and the link stay on every list | The owner owns the project's relationships and made the call |
| Refresh | The daily refresh runs the script (one request); a sheet the script cannot read halts the step and files its report like any other | ADR-023 |

## Amended 2026-11-13: one letter, two meanings

The 13 November refresh held the sheet on a branch: its legend had gained an `S` of its own ("Good Fit, but needs vehicle support for lifting", on the Galaxy's ground vehicles) beside the 890 Jump's `S/N` ("Not / Safe to Land"), and the lookup gave every `S` the new words, the 890 Jump's included.

- Where a mark has its own entry and a pair names it too, the pair's words stand on a carrier column that also uses the pair's other letter (the 890 Jump has both S and N), and the mark's own entry everywhere else (`noteFor` in `apps/web/lib/fitting.ts`). The rule reads the sheet; no carrier is named in code.
- Words that open with another verdict's own words (a "Good Fit, but…" on a red cell, as the Starlancer's one `S` is) would contradict the cell's colour, so the mark is shown as written, like a mark the legend does not explain.

## Consequences

- `packages/schema` gains `CargoBox` and `CargoBoxes`; `fixtures/cargo/` holds the shapes and the script's sample; `apps/web/lib/cargo.ts` and `showcase/cargo-grids.tsx` draw the section; the refresh gains the `cargo-boxes` step.
- The particulars' Cargo row and the Crew and operations fact are unchanged; the compare page is unchanged.
- The fitting list: `packages/schema` gains `ShipFitList`; `fixtures/fitting/` holds the list and the parser's sample; `scripts/fitting/` the reader, the script and `names.json`; `apps/web/lib/fitting.ts` the index and `fitting-lists.tsx` the lists; CLAUDE.md section 2 names the exception; the refresh gains the `fitting` step; `fflate` joins the root dev dependencies.
- From the audit of the fitting-list PR, taken: links lead to a ship's entry, never its Display or Collector edition; the pack's name stands where a label names one entry, the sheet's label as written where it names several or none; the sheet's "UNDER CONSTRUCTION" band and its date are read and said under the lists, and the page says "the list for build …", not "tested on"; the about page carries the credit; CLAUDE.md section 2 names the fabricator row and section 9 the sheet's one request a day.
- Deferred from that audit: the 890 Jump's S and N marks share the legend's one "Not / Safe to Land" sentence, printed beside each; the legend lists `***` twice (a cell mark and a heading tier) and the first stands; the Cargo section's aside names the pack's LIVE build over a list for another; an unmatched label or a contradictory cell is logged, not a failure of the refresh step; the "UNRELEASED" heading is looked for only in the row above the carriers, and the Starlancer's hangar column is read by the Freelancer MAX carrying no marker; a tick written with a variation selector would read as a mark; the merged spaces text tests by substring; tests for the variant trimming, the merged spaces and the date halt; the "SHIP SERVICES" row (colour only, no legend); the carriers' heading colours, which the legend reads as the ship's physical-components tier, are not read (two yellows, one in the legend); the marks the legend does not explain (SRC, Front, v) are shown as written; an unreleased carrier (the Odin, the Kraken) has a concept page but no class name, so its fits are not listed there yet.
- Deferred: a 24 SCU box is in the shapes but no grid in the pack names 24 as its largest box, so it appears in the table and the picker and stands where the record allows a 32; the grids' `min_size` and `max_size` caps are not read until their axes are understood; the vehicle's own inventory (`vehicle_inventory`, `inventory_containers`) is not shown, its unit being unstated in the record.
