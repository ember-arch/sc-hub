# ADR-038: The hero home page: dispatches first, featured ships that change daily, the developers' latest, this year's funding

- Status: accepted; the featured-post rule is refined by ADR-039 (staff-opened threads first, then the threads staff answered in, newest first); the funding card's link to the ledger ends with ADR-040, which folds the ledger into the card; the top of the page is a show's hero while one runs (ADR-057) and the lead story otherwise (ADR-058), the daily line of ADR-042 retired
- Date: 2026-11-10
- Deciders: owner ("on the home page - let's move dispatches to the top of the page under the stats and call them 'Dispatches from the Verse'. Then under that the Ships Hero section we have now labeled Featured Vehicles and Ships (updating daily) with a 'See All XXX Ships and Vehicles in the register' link below that (removing the broader entries A-Z block and replacing it with Featured Developer posts from Spectrum (maybe the top 3-5 from the past 48 hours) and then below that an always updating one visualization for Funding that Shows Total Funding YTD (with % to last year's total funding and current YoY growth) and a toggle that switches to YTD monthly funding with YoY monthly comparisons -- this will be our new Hero home page --- always relevant always updating and creating great hooks to dive deeper in the site", 2026-11-10), Claude Code session 2026-11-10
- Amends: ADR-020 and ADR-021 (the front page's order and parts), ADR-029 (where the front page's dispatches sit), system.md's Home row, spec 06

## Context

The front page of ADR-020 and ADR-021 was the almanac's front: the date, the title and the contents, the figure strip, the bento of five ship cards, the first ten entries A to Z, and the three newest dispatches last. It was a table of contents with pictures. With the site live (ADR-033), its pictures on the CDN (ADR-034), the news pack refreshed every two hours and the catalog and funding counters every day (ADR-023, ADR-027), and developer posts reproduced in full (ADR-037), the owner asked for a front page that is current every time it is built and leads into every section: dispatches first, the ships as a featured set that changes daily, the developers' latest posts, and this year's funding as one chart with a toggle.

Constraints: the pack is the only source (CLAUDE.md section 2: nothing hand-entered); Spectrum shows only with `SPECTRUM_MODE` on (ADR-027); one accent, tokens only, sentence case, tabular figures, at most three type sizes per page besides interface text, captions and labels (section 7); Lighthouse performance and accessibility at 90 or more on the home (section 6); the page is prerendered at build and rebuilt on every push to main, which the news run makes every two hours and the daily refresh every day.

## Decision

| Concern | Choice | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Order | The date, the title and the contents, then the figure strip, as before; then "Dispatches from the Verse"; then "Featured vehicles and ships"; then "Featured developer posts"; then "Funding this year"; the builds stay in the footer's patch strip (ADR-007) | The owner's order, top to bottom |
| Dispatches from the Verse | The three newest dispatches as `DispatchCard`s with their covers (ADR-029), moved from the bottom to under the strip, under the owner's title, with "All dispatches" at the right linking `/news`. The first cover loads with priority: it is now the page's first picture, and usually its largest paint; the bento's first card no longer carries `fetchPriority="high"` and loads like the rest | The owner's words; one picture with priority, the one nearest the top |
| Featured vehicles and ships | The bento (ADR-021) under the heading "Featured vehicles and ships" with "Changes daily" as its label at the right; under it one link, "See all N ships and vehicles in the register", N the pack's count of ships and concept ships (the same figure as the strip's "Ships on record"), to `/ships`. The showcase pair keep their cards: the live example large, the concept hero tall (ADR-010, the owner's choice). The three cards across change daily: `dailyPicks` in `apps/web/lib/front.ts` draws them from the ships with a picture (from every ship when none has one, so CI keeps the layout) by a shuffle seeded with the UTC day number of the server's clock (`now()`, the same clock as the date line), so every build on one day shows the same three and the next day's build shows others. The entries A to Z and their price credit leave the front page | The owner's "updating daily"; a seeded draw is deterministic, so a build is reproducible and a test can check it; the pair stays because ADR-010 made it the owner's choice, not a ranking |
| Featured developer posts | Under "Featured developer posts", with "All developer posts" at the right linking `/news/dev`: the Spectrum posts of the past 48 hours (from `now()`), one card per thread, the thread's newest post, threads ordered by that post, five at most; when fewer than three threads posted in the window, the newest remaining posts fill the section to three, still one per thread (`featuredPosts` in `apps/web/lib/front.ts`). "Top" is recency by thread: Spectrum's votes and reply counts are not on the pack, and a thread with several staff replies in a day (patch notes, a contest) would otherwise fill the section with one conversation. The cards are `DevPost`s as the tracker shows them (ADR-035, ADR-037): folded, the bodies through `renderBody` with the pictures from the manifest, the links to the post and the thread always visible. With Spectrum off the section is not rendered, like every Spectrum surface; with it on and nothing on the pack, the tracker's empty state | The owner's "top 3-5 from the past 48 hours", made exact with what the pack has |
| Funding this year | Under "Funding this year", with "The ledger of pledges" at the right linking `/funding`: one card. Across its top, three figures: pledged this year so far, the share of last year's full total it has reached ("of 2025's total"), and the change on the same days of last year ("year on year"), all from `headline` and `yearRows` in `apps/web/lib/funding.ts` (`yearAgainstLastYear`). Under them a toggle of two views. "Year to date": the funding page's `YearVsYear`, this year's running total in the accent against last year's in ink, so the gap to last year's end is the drawing. "By month": `MonthPairs`, each month from January to the current one as a pair of bars, last year's in the ink's 35 percent tint (one of the month grid's tints, ADR-020) and this year's in the accent (the current month at 50 percent, as the ledger draws the current year's bar, ADR-018, and marked "so far"), on the ledger's graph paper in its frame, the change on the same month last year under each pair from the `md` breakpoint and in every bar's title, except the current month, which is not over and is not read against a whole one (`monthPairs` in `lib/funding.ts`). The toggle is a client control, `ViewToggle` in `apps/web/app/view-toggle.tsx`: two buttons that read as one control in the segmented control's own classes, `aria-pressed` on the current one; the server renders both views and the "Year to date" view shows until the reader presses the other | The owner's two views; the funding page's own measures and drawing (ADR-018, ADR-020), never a second source; a client toggle keeps the home a static page, where URL state would make it render per request |
| Nothing new in the tokens | No colour, size, face, spacing or radius is added. The bars use the ink's 35 percent tint (the month grid's, ADR-020) and the accent at 50 percent (the current year's bar, ADR-018); the frame, graph paper and labels are the ledger's | CLAUDE.md section 2 |
| Copy | "Dispatches from the Verse" keeps the game's own name for its universe capitalised, as a proper noun; everything else is sentence case | system.md section 5 |

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Rank the developer posts by thread activity from `fixtures/spectrum/thread-state.json` | That file records which posts the thread step has read, not Spectrum's votes or replies; it is not a measure of what readers found interesting |
| One card per post, newest first | Eight posts in the last 48 hours on 2026-11-10 came from five threads; two were the same contest thread and two the same patch-notes thread |
| Rotate the showcase pair too | ADR-010 made the pair the owner's choice; this record keeps it until he says otherwise |
| Choose the daily ships by a rule (longest, newest, most recently changed) | A rule shows the same ships every day; the owner asked for a set that changes |
| URL state for the funding toggle (`/?view=months`) | Would make the home page render per request for one control; the funding page's own `SegmentedLinks` are the right pattern where a page already takes query state |
| Twelve months with the rest of the year empty | The owner asked for year to date; the empty months would be a forecast's shape |
| Keep the entries A to Z under the bento | The owner removed them; the register link carries the count |

## Consequences

- `apps/web/app/page.tsx` is rebuilt in the order above; `apps/web/app/front.tsx` gains `RegisterLink` and keeps `Contents`, `Counters`, `Bento` and `RunningHead`; `apps/web/app/funding-now.tsx` (`FundingNow`, `MonthPairs`) and `apps/web/app/view-toggle.tsx` (`ViewToggle`) are new; `FigureStrip` takes `phoneColumns`, so the card's three short figures sit in one row on a phone; `apps/web/app/price-credit.tsx` stays for the register and compare pages.
- `apps/web/lib/front.ts` gains `dailyPicks`, `featuredPosts` and `FEATURED_WINDOW_HOURS`; `apps/web/lib/funding.ts` gains `yearAgainstLastYear` and `monthPairs`; unit tests cover both on synthetic rows and the committed fixtures.
- The home records change (`e2e/screens.spec.ts`); `e2e/devtracker.spec.ts` gains the home with Spectrum on (`home-populated`), which CI does not run, so those records come from a local run, as the tracker's do.
- system.md's Home row and its line on the front page's parts, and spec 06, say what the front page is now. ADR-029's sentence on the front page's dispatches still holds; they have moved up.
- The page is as current as its last build: the news run rebuilds it every two hours, the daily refresh every day; a day with neither keeps the previous day's date line and featured ships, as before.
- A post without a thread id on the pack is keyed by its thread's address instead (`threadUrl`'s rule), so one thread never fills the section either way. A month in which RSI lowered the counter (ADR-018) draws as no bar, its figure in the title and the summary.
- Deferred: a reader without JavaScript sees the "Year to date" view only; `ViewToggle` repeats the segmented control's classes rather than sharing them from `packages/ui`; the "By month" view's per-month change labels show from `md`, phones have them in the bars' titles and the card's figures.
