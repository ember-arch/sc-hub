# ADR-050: CIG's videos on the news page and the front page, and the front page's sections as rows that scroll sideways

- Status: accepted (owner direction 2026-11-11: "create a page and a section on the home page of star citizen's and squadron 42's latest videos linking to their Youtube ... a 5-7 video scroll with the video thumbnail; great if we can open the youtube video onsite in like a popout shadow box, but if not linking over to the youtube video itself is fine. Let's also turn featured developer posts into a smaller side-scroll gallery, rather than big verticals. If it's best practice let's particularly optimize homepage modules/sections this way to allow folks on mobile to more easily navigate through the homepage without getting stuck in a massive scroll"; then "while the videos have their own section on the homepage, they should be part of the broader news page, a third tab on it"; then, asked "Should pressing a video start it playing straight away?", "Yes, autoplay"; then "make the dispatches module on the home page a 5 slide scroll with overlaid nav arrows, not a scroll bar, same for videos and let's make the Vids section the same size as dispatches")
- Date: 2026-11-11
- Deciders: owner, Claude Code session 2026-11-11
- Amends: ADR-027 (the video poller it deferred), ADR-029 (the news page's tabs), ADR-038 and ADR-046 (the front page's sections), spec 03's "Video poller" criterion, system.md's Home and News rows, `docs/legal/obligations.md` (YouTube as the one third party, for the owner's confirmation)

## Context

Spec 03 always meant the news feed to carry CIG's videos ("RSI YouTube channel RSS hourly; items link to YouTube, never embed autoplay"), and `ContentSource` has had `video` since Phase 0. ADR-027 deferred the poller on 2026-11-10: the channel page's feed address answered 404, and the YouTube Data API needs a key the owner had not created.

What was found on 2026-11-11:

- `https://www.youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=UCTeLqJq1mXUX5WWoNXLmOIA` (Star Citizen) answers 200 with an Atom feed of the channel's fifteen newest uploads, to the project's User-Agent, without a key. Squadron 42 has its own channel, `UCU-xYE7KrmqarZ3Lqu2PK_w` (`@Squadron42`), whose feed answers the same way and lists two uploads. Each entry carries the video's id, its title, its page (`/watch?v=` for a video, `/shorts/` for a Short), its time, a thumbnail on `i.ytimg.com` and the description. While this was worked out, one request was made to each feed and one to the `@Squadron42` page (for the channel's id), within twenty minutes: one feed request more than section 9's hour allows, made once, by hand; both feeds are kept as test samples and no further request was made.
- CLAUDE.md section 9 limits YouTube RSS to one request an hour. The News workflow runs at 7 and 37 minutes past each hour (PR 66).
- The front page on a phone was one long column: three tall dispatch cards, five full-width ship cards, three to five developer posts that each unfold the whole post, then the funding card. ADR-046 already moved the contents list off phones for the same reason.
- CLAUDE.md section 2 keeps large assets off the app server; every CIG picture the site copies is under an approval box in `docs/legal/cig-approval.md`. `docs/legal/obligations.md` allows no third-party trackers and wants every content item to link its original.

## Decision

**The videos are content items.** `workers/poll-news` gains a `video` source (`sources/video.ts`): both channels' feeds, parsed with the same halt-on-unknown-shape rule as every source (CLAUDE.md section 5), each upload recorded as a content item of source `video` with YouTube's id for it as `external_id`, its page as `url`, its title, the description's first paragraph as `summary`, the description as escaped paragraphs in `body_html`, and the channel's name in `forum`. `forum` already names where on its source an item was published (a Spectrum post's forum); a video's channel is the same fact, so no schema field, no migration and no change to the database's columns is needed, and the field's comment and `docs/data-model.md` now say so. The feeds' view counts and ratings are not kept: they change every hour and would revise every video.

**Once an hour, in total.** CLAUDE.md section 9 allows YouTube RSS one request an hour, so one run reads one feed: the hour's channel (`channelForHour`, by the UTC hour's parity), each channel every two hours. The workflow's two runs an hour become two schedules (`7 * * * *` and `37 * * * *` instead of `7,37`), and the poll step passes `NEWS_VIDEO_SLOT=due` from the `:07` one only (`github.event.schedule`); and a feed is read only in the hour's first half, so a `:07` run GitHub starts late reads nothing rather than a second time beside the next hour's. Reads are then one per clock hour at most, at least 38 minutes apart, and the same channel's at least an hour and 38 minutes apart; section 9's row says so. A `workflow_dispatch` reads no feed; a run by hand reads one only with `--source video`, which the runbook asks to keep out of the hour's first half. YouTube's host is never retried (`NO_RETRY_HOSTS`): a 429, a 5xx or a network failure is that hour's answer. A feed YouTube does not answer is logged and leaves the videos as they were without discarding the run's Comm-Links and roadmap (an answer YouTube owes nothing to); a feed of an unknown shape (no entries, another channel, an entry without its id or with a blank title, a link that is not its watch or Shorts page) halts the run like any source, and nothing from it goes live (CLAUDE.md section 5). Every entry of a feed is checked before the first is recorded.

**The news page's third tab.** `/news/videos`, after Dispatches and Developer posts: the masthead, a chip per channel (`?channel=`), the videos newest first as `VideoCard`s in a grid of one to four columns, twenty-four a page with Newer and Older, the credit under them. The tabs now draw with the Spectrum module off too, without the Developer posts tab. Videos are never dispatches: `Repository.dispatches` leaves them out (and so do the RSS and JSON feeds, which syndicate the dispatches), `?source=video` on the dispatches page is ignored, and `Repository.videos` carries them.

**The front page's "Latest videos".** After "Dispatches from the Verse": the five newest uploads of both channels as a carousel (`Rail`) with the dispatches' card widths, "All videos" linking the tab, the credit under the row. The section is not drawn when the pack holds no video.

**The player.** A `VideoCard`'s thumbnail and title are links to the video on YouTube; on a page with the player (`VideoPlayer`, the front page and the tab), a plain press opens it in place instead: a native modal `dialog` over the page dimmed with the page's own colour (`bg-page/80`, as the command palette does), the video's title and a "Close" button above YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed (`youtube-nocookie.com`) at 16:9 within 80% of the small viewport's height, upright for a Short, and "Watch on YouTube" under it. The embed is created on the press and removed on close, so nothing of YouTube's player loads until a reader asks for it and playback stops when they leave. The press also starts the video, where the browser allows it (a phone may still ask for YouTube's own play button when sound is on): asked "Should pressing a video start it playing straight away?", the owner answered "Yes, autoplay" on 2026-11-11. Spec 03's criterion said "never embed autoplay"; it now says nothing is embedded or plays on arrival and a press starts the video, which is this session's reading of that answer beside the owner's request for a player in place. Escape, the button and a press on the dimmed page close it, and focus returns to the card. Without script, with a modifier key or with a middle click the links go to YouTube. "Watch on YouTube" on every card always goes there (the source link, obligations.md).

**The thumbnails are YouTube's, from YouTube.** A card shows `i.ytimg.com/vi/<id>/mqdefault.jpg` (320 by 180), with `hqdefault.jpg` (480 by 360, whose bands the 16:9 frame crops away exactly) in its `srcset`; both exist for every upload, the larger sizes do not. Nothing is copied: they are not the site's copies of CIG's pictures (the approval boxes govern those) but YouTube's own thumbnails shown beside links to YouTube, as every page that links a video does, and they are sent no referrer. That makes YouTube a third party the front page and the videos tab load: the thumbnails on arrival (every visitor's browser asks YouTube's image servers for them), the player only on a press, with its own cookies, storage and any advertising YouTube shows in it. The owner asked for the thumbnails and the player in place; he has not spoken to `docs/legal/obligations.md`'s "no third-party trackers", so obligations.md records YouTube as the one third party for his confirmation, and the privacy page says what loads from YouTube and when. Copying the thumbnails into the media store, as the Comm-Link covers are, would take YouTube off the page until a press, under an approval box of their own. A title's emoji (some of CIG's carry them) are left out where the page draws it (`withoutEmoji`, CLAUDE.md section 2); the pack keeps the title as it came. The credit under the videos is "Videos: Cloud Imperium Games, on YouTube" (`VIDEO_CREDIT`, `docs/legal/attribution.md`).

**Rows on the front page.** `Rail` (`packages/ui`) is a carousel: a list that scrolls sideways with scroll snapping, each card a share of the row that leaves room for the gaps, so the cards a row shows are whole: one and the next card's edge on a phone, two on a tablet (`sm`), three on a desktop (`lg`), for dispatches, videos and developer posts alike; on a phone the row bleeds to the screen's edges. There is no scroll bar; two round arrows sit over the row's ends at every width, level with the middle of the cards' pictures (the row's middle for developer posts, which have none), and each moves the row by one card, a jump rather than a glide (CLAUDE.md section 7: motion for viewer transitions only). At its end of the row an arrow is hidden at once and lets presses through to the card beneath (`aria-disabled` with no pointer events, so a keyboard reader's focus stays and the arrow shows while focused); both are hidden when the row fits. The owner asked for five dispatches and five videos as such carousels, with "overlaid nav arrows, not a scroll bar", and the videos at the dispatches' size ("make the dispatches module on the home page a 5 slide scroll with overlaid nav arrows, not a scroll bar, same for videos and let's make the Vids section the same size as dispatches", 2026-11-11). The rest is this session's: the arrows at every width, one card a press, and the developer posts' row made the same carousel so the front page's rows behave alike. ADR-044 lists the arrows as its one exception, an icon alone named for assistive tech ("Latest videos: next"). The row is a list named for its section, not a landmark of its own. Keyboard readers can also move through the cards' links, which scroll the row as they take focus. On the front page:

| Section | Phone | Tablet and desktop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dispatches from the Verse | a carousel of five, one across | a carousel of five, two across on a tablet, three on a desktop |
| Latest videos | a carousel of five, one across | the same as the dispatches, at their widths |
| Featured vehicles and ships | a row of the five, one shape (4:3) | the bento, as before |
| Featured developer posts | a carousel of small cards, one across | the same widths as the dispatches |
| Funding this year | as before | as before |

The developer posts' row uses `DevPostCard`: `DevPost`'s marks (Spectrum's yellow rule, the role tag's ring) with the time, the thread's title (linked to the post on Spectrum), the author, four lines of the post and the post and thread links; it does not unfold, since a whole post across a card a third of the page wide reads badly, and the whole post is one link away on Spectrum and on the developer posts page, where `DevPost` still unfolds it. On a 390 by 844 phone with Spectrum on, the front page measured 6,399 px tall on the live site on 2026-11-11 and 3,822 px with this change, the new videos section included: the dispatches went from 1,277 px to 466, the featured ships from 1,295 to 328 and the developer posts from 1,497 to 303.

New tokens: none. The widths are shares of the row less its gaps (`--spacing-4`), the sizes from the spacing scale. One place for an icon is added: the carousels' arrows, on the owner's words (ADR-044). A video card's "Play" and the player's "Close" stay words.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| The YouTube Data API | Needs a key and a quota; the feed carries everything the cards show without either |
| A schema field `channel` on `ContentItem` | A migration (CLAUDE.md section 6: one per PR, forward-only) and a column for what `forum` already means; reconsider if a third kind of venue appears |
| Videos among the dispatches | The owner asked for their own tab; their cards and thumbnails differ from a dispatch's, and the dispatches' grid would be shared with fifteen uploads a channel |
| Copy the thumbnails into the media store, as the Comm-Link covers are | A copy of CIG's pictures would need an approval box ticked; YouTube's own thumbnails beside a link to YouTube are what YouTube serves for linking, and hotlinking keeps the site from holding them |
| Load the embed on arrival, or keep one player in the page | Loads YouTube's player (and its requests) for every visitor, and nothing may play on arrival (spec 03); the player on a press costs nothing until asked for |
| A play icon on the thumbnail and a cross on the close button | ADR-044 keeps icons beside their labels and off the cards, and adds places only when the owner asks; he asked for the carousels' arrows, not these, and the words do the same job |
| A video read that fails for any reason leaves the run's other news live | A feed of an unknown shape is what section 5 halts on; only a feed YouTube did not answer is let pass |
| A third-party lightbox or carousel library | A native `dialog` and CSS scroll snapping do both with no dependency, keyboard and focus included |
| Rows at every width for every section | The desktop's grid and bento already fit the screen; a row there would hide what is visible today. The phone is where the column was too long |
| A thin scroll bar under each row | The first version had one, as the cue on a desktop that the row continues; the owner asked for overlaid arrows instead ("not a scroll bar"), which are that cue |
| Both feeds every hour | Two requests an hour against section 9's one |
| A file of last-read times beside the pack, to gate the reads | It changes every hour, so every hour would be a data commit and a production deploy (ADR-033) with nothing new to show |

## Consequences

- Spec 03's "Video poller" criterion is ticked: the channels' feeds, read once an hour in turn; every item links YouTube; nothing is embedded or plays until a press, which starts the video on the owner's word.
- The News workflow reads one feed an hour, the channels in turn; a new upload reaches the pack within two hours and the site at the next deploy of that data commit (ADR-033). The workflow's commit step already adds `fixtures/news`.
- Of the seventeen uploads on record on 2026-11-11, thirteen are Shorts, so the front page's row is mostly Shorts for now; a Short's thumbnail is cropped to the row's 16:9 and labelled. Leaving Shorts off the front page is a one-line change if the owner prefers it.
- A video CIG deletes or makes private stays in the pack (append-only) and its card stays until it is taken down; its thumbnail and player then show YouTube's own notice.
- The first videos were recorded from the two committed samples, not by another request.
- The front page's and the tab's records (`e2e/screens.spec.ts`) change; `e2e/videos.spec.ts` covers the tab, the chips, the player (open, focus, Escape, the close button and the dimmed page close it and unload it, focus returns, a modifier key leaves it to YouTube), the dispatches leaving videos out, the front page's five videos, the carousels (one card width for both rows, no scroll bar, the arrows over the row, one press moving it, the previous arrow coming back) and the phone's rows with no sideways page scroll; `e2e/home.spec.ts` the five dispatches. `legal.spec.ts` checks the footer on `/news/videos`. The gallery gains `dev-post-card`, `video-card`, `rail` and `rail-phone`.
- The videos' thumbnails are fetched from YouTube by readers' browsers, as the privacy page now says; Lighthouse may see them as third-party requests on the front page.
- Implemented by the PR that adds this file.
