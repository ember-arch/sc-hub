# ADR-057: Events: one format for every show, read from RSI's own schedule, at the top of the front page while it runs

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-12
- Deciders: owner (an IAE page that "updates daily with each new manufacturer", graphical, with lots of pictures, "absolutely gorgeous to look at (since it is a marquee event)", covering where and what is happening, what the manufacturers say, the new ships on sale and the hull-limited sales and when; a banner at or near the top of the site for the length of the event, gone the day after it ends; the canonical format for every major Star Citizen and Squadron 42 show, IAE the first; and, in this session: "instead of this text block we make this the hero block for this event and then after it, the space becomes a image-based hero block for the most relevant news, video, or post etc that updates daily or every couple fo days", 2026-11-12), Claude Code session 2026-11-12
- Amends: ADR-038 and ADR-042 (what stands at the top of the front page while a show runs), ADR-027 (the News workflow gains a step), CLAUDE.md sections 4 and 9

## Context

The Intergalactic Aerospace Expo 2956 opens on 13 November 2026 at 16:00 UTC and runs to 27 November, at the Tobin Expo Center in New Babbage, microTech. What RSI published about it, and when, as found on 12 November (the session's notes keep the detail):

- 6 November: Comm-Link 21389, a countdown page whose schedule is a picture; a Spectrum post with the dates.
- 9 November: Comm-Link 21386, "Free Fly and Manufacturer Schedule": the show's introduction, then one entry per day (Day 1 Roberts Space Industries, Day 2 Origin, … Day 9 Aopoa, Banu, Esperia and Gatac, Day 10 Best in Show, Days 11 to 15 the finale), each with RSI's words for the day, the ships it puts on the floor as links to their pledge pages with one line each (192 in all), and a banner picture named for one of them ("galaxy2026", "m80", "carrack2026" …).
- 10 November: the staff FAQ on Spectrum (thread 575984, three revisions in the pack by 11 November): the hours, the hall, the rentals, Best in Show, and the stock-limited sale: six waves at 16:00, 20:00, 00:00, 04:00, 08:00 and 12:00 UTC, and the hulls by the day their waves start (the 890 Jump from Day 2, the Kraken and Kraken Privateer from Day 5, the Pioneer from Day 6, the Idris-P and Javelin from Day 8), with the Prospector's price rise on MISC's day.
- Through the show: new reveals "throughout the event", Comm-Links most days, Warbond deals of 24 hours that are not announced ahead.

The schedule Comm-Link is made with RSI's page builder: the transmission page is an app shell, as every Comm-Link is (ADR-027), but its HTML names, in an entity-encoded JSON, the address of its body (`content.htmlUrl`, under `/alexandria/html/`, a content-addressed name that changes when RSI edits the page), and the body is server-made component markup with the text, the links and the pictures in it. Two requests read the whole schedule. The Star Citizen Wiki's copy of the Comm-Link holds only its first sentence.

Constraints: nothing hand-entered (CLAUDE.md section 2); RSI at one request every 5 seconds with the contact address (section 9); CIG's text quoted only briefly, with attribution; the page is prerendered at build, so it is as current as its last build (ADR-038); the pictures come from the media manifest (ADR-034), never from RSI's host directly; Spectrum items reach a page only with `SPECTRUM_MODE` on (ADR-027); tokens only, at most three type sizes besides interface text, captions and labels (section 7).

## Decision

### The record

| Concern | Choice | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What an event is | `EventRecord` in `packages/schema/event.ts`: the slug and kind (`iae`, `invictus`, `citizencon`, `other`), RSI's title and subtitle, the opening moment and the last day, the venue (hall, city, body, system), RSI's opening sentence, the key art's address, the days, the sale, the sources and the body's address. A day: RSI's day numbers (`Day 11-15` is 11 to 15), its title, its makers (RSI's headings on a shared day, the title on a one-maker day, none on Best in Show or the finale), RSI's own dates, its hours (RSI's dates at the show's daily opening time), RSI's first sentence for it, its ships (RSI's name, the pledge link, RSI's line cut to its first sentence, the maker heading it stands under), the ship its banner shows and the banner's address. The sale: the waves (number, hour, RSI's next-day mark), the hulls (RSI's name, first day, the show's day number) and RSI's sentences on price changes | One shape for any show RSI lays out in days, with every field a fact RSI states; a fact it does not state stays null |
| Which shows | `fixtures/events/registry.json`, one row per show: the slug, the kind, the schedule Comm-Link's id and the FAQ thread's id. The one file of the module kept by hand, and it holds pointers, never facts | RSI names its schedule pages differently each year; a pointer added when RSI announces a show is honest, a guess at which Comm-Link is a schedule is not |
| Where it is read | `scripts/events/fetch.ts` (logic in `parse.ts`, `build.ts` and `run.ts`): for each show in the registry, the Comm-Link's publication time and the FAQ's latest revision from the news pack, then RSI's transmission page (asked at `/en/`, so without the redirect) and the body it names, 5 seconds apart, the first request too, with the project User-Agent. A component, a day label, a list line or a FAQ wave line it does not recognise halts the run and nothing is written (CLAUDE.md section 5). The day's hours: RSI's dates ("November 13-14" opens on the 13th and closes on the 14th; "November 22" closes a day later; "Day 11-15, November 23-27" names the first and last days' own dates) at the opening hour RSI gives ("November 13, 16:00 UTC"), the finale cut at the end of the last day. The year: the Comm-Link's, or the next or the previous when the month is more than half a year away | The source and the rate of the cover step (ADR-051); the parsing rules are the page's own structure, tested on the real pages (`fixtures/events/samples/`) |
| When RSI is asked | Only when a show is new, or its phase has changed since it was last read: `before`, `opening` (its first UTC day, before the doors), `day-N`, `after`. `fixtures/events/state.json` records the phase of the last read. In practice two requests when a show first appears, then two each day at the hour the day turns over, which is when RSI's line-up for the day goes live; once a show is over and read so, never again. A run that is not due asks RSI nothing and reads the FAQ's sale from the pack again | Twenty-odd requests over a fortnight; the day the page needs re-reading is the day the show moves on |
| How it reaches the site | The step runs in the News workflow, after the Spectrum poll and before the covers (`continue-on-error`, five minutes at most); the commit step stages `fixtures/events` and names "the shows" in its message when they changed. The news clock starts the workflow at 7 and 37 past the hour (ADR-053), so the first run after a day turns over (16:07 for IAE 2956) reads RSI, the state file's phase changes, and the push redeploys the site with the new day, whether RSI changed its page or not. A record whose data did not change is not rewritten (stamps aside, ADR-023's rule) | The News workflow is the cadence that matters for a day that turns at 16:00; the daily refresh runs once in the morning. The state file is the honest trigger: the show has moved on, so the site does |
| Without the contact address | The step stands aside and says so; nothing is written | CLAUDE.md section 9 |

### The pages

| Concern | Choice | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The event page | `/events/<slug>`, the canonical format: the opening band (CIG's picture of the day's featured ship, full width and rounded, the status and the credit over its top, the show's name, its short name and dates and the day on the floor over the lower part on the scrim, links to today's ships, the limited sales and the schedule); the strip of days under it (one chip per entry with its label, dates and RSI's short title, the day on now selected, each an in-page `Link` to its day); the facts (when, where, on the floor, limited sales); "On the floor today" (before the doors open, "First on the floor"): the makers with their marks where the fankit has one, RSI's sentence for the day quoted and linked, the day's hours and the hulls whose sale starts that day, and every ship of the day as a picture tile into its entry; "Limited sales": the waves as a ruler of the hours in UTC with the waves on the next calendar day said so, the hulls by their first day as picture tiles, RSI's sentences on price changes quoted; "The schedule": every day as a section with its banner picture and its credit, its makers, its status (over, on now, next or its opening moment), its limited hulls, RSI's sentence and its ships as a list into their entries; "From the show floor": RSI's Comm-Links and videos that name the show, and every Comm-Link published while it runs, as dispatch cards with their covers; "Sources" | The owner's five questions (where and what, what the makers say, the ships, the limited sales, when), in that order, with the pictures the register already holds; one page per show, the same parts every year |
| `/events` | Every show on file, the latest first, each a picture card into its page | A home for the format; the sitemap lists both |
| The front page | From the UTC day a show opens to the end of its last day, its hero stands in the place of the daily line and the contents (`FrontEvent`, `apps/web/app/front-event.tsx`): the show's short name as the page's title over the day's featured picture, the day and its makers, links to the event page, to today's ships and, when a hull's sale starts that day, to the limited sales, and the strip of days under it. The date line stays above it. The first dispatch cover gives up its loading priority to the hero's picture. The day after the show's last day the front page is as before | The owner's "banner ... for the length of the event" and "instead of this text block ... the hero block for this event"; `eventOnFront` is one pure rule the site, the step and the tests share |
| Pictures | Through the media manifest (ADR-034), as every picture on the site: a day's banner is its featured ship's own opening picture (`media().showcaseCard`, the picture its ship page opens on, which the wiki's galleries or RSI's art supply), else the first of its ships with one; a ship's tile is its card picture (`shipCardImage`, RSI's ship-matrix picture, or the gallery's); concepts their ship-matrix picture. Each picture carries its credit. The featured ship is the one RSI's banner for the day names, by the banner's file name, so the picture is of the ship RSI chose for the day. Without media (CI) the band and the tiles stand as toned panels with their words | The register already holds pictures of every ship on the schedule at up to 5,120 px; RSI's own event art would need a new media path, deferred below |
| Matching ships to the register | By the pledge page the schedule links (the ship matrix's page for each ship, `fixtures/rsi-media/sources.json`, and each concept's page on its record), preferring an entry over its display copy or an edition; else by name, RSI's name ending in the register's ("RSI Aurora Mk II" is the Aurora Mk II), the longest such name winning. All 192 ships of IAE 2956 resolve, 185 by their page; a ship neither finds keeps RSI's link | RSI's own link is the surest key; the name rule covers the store links RSI uses for a few |
| Makers | RSI's heading matched to the register's manufacturer by name, "and" and "&" alike, or by code ("BANU"); the register's name and mark shown, else RSI's capitals set as a name | The makers have pages here; RSI writes some headings in capitals |
| The sale and Spectrum | The sale comes from a Spectrum post, so it shows only with the Spectrum module on, like every Spectrum surface (ADR-027); without it the page has no limited-sales section and the hero no sale link | ADR-027's rule that nothing from Spectrum reaches a page by accident |
| RSI's words | Each quote is RSI's first sentence of a day or a ship, or a price sentence, never more, in quotation marks and linked to its page; RSI's exclamation marks stay inside its quotes, the site's own copy has none | CLAUDE.md: CIG text only briefly, with attribution; system.md's copy rules |
| Type | The title in `display` and `hero`, names and facts in `heading`, quotes in `body`, the rest `ui`, captions and labels: no new token, colour, size or radius | CLAUDE.md section 7 |
| A pinned clock | Off Vercel, `SITE_CLOCK` (an ISO time) pins `now()`, so a local build can be photographed on any day of a show; a deployment always reads its own clock | The e2e records of the running show come from a local run; CI builds on the day it runs |

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Type the schedule into a fixture by hand | CLAUDE.md section 2; RSI changes the page as reveals land |
| The wiki's copy of the Comm-Link | One sentence of it |
| Read RSI every run of the News workflow | Forty-eight pairs of requests a day for a page that changes with the day; the phase rule asks twice a day at most |
| Run the step in the daily refresh | 06:23 UTC is ten hours before the day turns; the front page would show yesterday's maker for most of the day |
| A client-side clock that moves the hero to the next day in the reader's browser | Two sources of truth for the day, a flash on load and the whole schedule in the client bundle; the state file makes the build follow the day instead |
| Incremental regeneration (`revalidate`) for the home and the event page | The pages read the committed packs from the file system at build; a regenerating function would have to carry them, which nothing on the site does yet, and the deploy behaviour cannot be tested before a preview |
| Hide the days not yet open, revealing each on its day | RSI publishes the whole line-up ahead; hiding it would withhold a fact. The page leads with the day on now and shows the rest as next and later |
| A separate banner above the masthead | The owner asked for the hero in the text block's place |

## Consequences

- New: `packages/schema/event.ts` (`EventSource`, `EventRecord`, `eventPhase`, `phaseKey`, `eventOnFront`); `scripts/events/` (`parse.ts`, `build.ts`, `run.ts`, `fetch.ts`) with `scripts/__tests__/events.test.ts` on the real pages; `fixtures/events/` (`registry.json`, `iae-2956.json`, `state.json`, `samples/`, a README); `apps/web/lib/events.ts` with `apps/web/__tests__/events.test.ts`; `apps/web/app/events/` (`page.tsx`, `[slug]/page.tsx`, `parts.tsx`); `apps/web/app/front-event.tsx`; `apps/web/e2e/events.spec.ts`; the event page and the index in `screens.spec.ts`; the event page in `lighthouserc.json`.
- `.github/workflows/news.yml` gains the step and stages `fixtures/events`. `docs/runbooks/events.md` says how to add a show (one registry row when RSI publishes its schedule and FAQ) and how to read a halt.
- The home's e2e tests of the daily line and the contents skip while a show is on, saying why; `events.spec.ts` checks the hero in their place and its absence otherwise.
- The front page changes at the first build of a show's first UTC day and again at the first build of the day after its last: the news clock's run at 00:07 UTC records the phase and redeploys. A day with no run keeps the previous build, as ADR-038 says of the date line.
- RSI's own event art (the key art and the day banners named in the record) is not yet on the site: it needs a path into the media store like the covers' (ADR-051), with the hand publish carrying it too. A follow-up; until then the record keeps their addresses and the pages show the register's pictures of the same ships.
- After a show, the owner's next ask stands: the same space becomes a picture-led hero for the most relevant dispatch, video or post, changing daily or every couple of days. That is its own decision and pull request; this one leaves the daily line and the contents in place outside a show.
- Not handled: a show whose schedule is not a page-builder Comm-Link halts on its first read and needs a reader for its shape; RSI's Warbond deals are not on the record, since RSI does not publish them ahead; local times are not shown, every hour is in UTC, as RSI gives them.
